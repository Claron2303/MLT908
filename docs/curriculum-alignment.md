# Food and water curriculum alignment

Primary classroom reference: Food and Water Insecurities in Singapore.pdf, supplied by the user (3 pages). Its final offer to create flashcards is source text, not a task instruction.

- Page 1: limited freshwater and catchment land, growing demand, drought, Four National Taps, energy-efficient water treatment. Applied to the Round 2 water lesson, reflections and expert replies.
- Page 2: floating solar, multipurpose water infrastructure, import dependence, export restrictions, logistics disruptions, extreme weather and import diversification. Applied to food slides and the water-to-Round-3 bridge.
- Page 3: space-efficient farming, closed-containment aquaculture, local buffers and growing overseas. Applied to pre-Round-4 teaching and reflection feedback.

## Context and corrections

The Three Food Baskets and 30 by 30 are presented as the earlier framework described in the notes. Current policy is shown separately: Singapore Food Story 2, announced November 2025, has four pillars. SFA's 2025 statistics explain the revised 2035 local production goals.

Sources:
- https://www.sfa.gov.sg/about-us/our-singapore-food-story-2
- https://www.sfa.gov.sg/news-publications/newsroom/singapore-food-statistics-2025
- https://www.sg101.gov.sg/economy/case-studies/sg-food-challenge/

The notes' Linggiu figures mix reservoir levels with percentage declines. The lesson retains the verified September 2019 case: capacity fell from 72% at the start of the year to below 50% in September. It does not describe this as a 50% decline, repeat the unverified June 2016 figure or present historical conditions as current.

## Teaching versus game rules

All four reflections remain reflective choices, with response-specific explanations; no new grading is introduced. Existing journal answers remain unchanged. The map continues to model farms, catchments and imports. It does not add NEWater, desalination, actual supply disruptions, overseas farms or stockpiles as playable systems. The 50% production upgrade and liveability bonus are classroom parameters. Videos, round progression, cleared maps and timers are retained.
